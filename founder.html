A personal introduction

Service, with intention.

For nearly two decades, Michelle Giray has worked at the intersection of service excellence, international operations and complex problem-solving.

Her career has involved leading diverse teams, overseeing service delivery across industries and creating systems that turn complicated processes into clear, accountable outcomes.

Through that experience, one principle has remained constant: identifying a problem is only the beginning. What matters is ensuring that someone takes responsibility for what happens next.

Michelle established TalentBuzz Private to bring that same discipline and personal attention to individuals and families who value their time, privacy and peace of mind.

As Founder and Private Client Director, she personally leads client relationships, supported by an operations team responsible for approved coordination, research and follow-through. Every engagement begins with an understanding of the client's preferences, boundaries and expectations.
