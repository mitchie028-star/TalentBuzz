Privacy & discretion

Confidence in the details. Respect for the personal.

From a travel itinerary to a household concern, the smallest details can be deeply personal. We believe attentive coordination should also be considered coordination—clear permissions, purposeful sharing, and thoughtful oversight.

Here is how TalentBuzz Private currently approaches the protection of client information.

01

Restricted client records

Confidential client files are maintained in a dedicated SharePoint workspace with permissions restricted to the Founder at present.

02

Protected business accounts

Our Microsoft 365 environment uses security defaults and multifactor authentication, adding safeguards to account access.

03

Controlled access

Access to sensitive information is granted only when approved and appropriate to the task. Our support team does not currently have access to confidential client records.

04

Your decisions remain yours

Purchases, financial commitments, bookings, and disclosures of sensitive information require specific client authorization.

05

Purposeful coordination

When external providers are needed, we aim to share only the details necessary for an authorized arrangement.

06

Founder-led oversight

Michelle Giray personally oversees private client relationships and approves access to confidential records as the business develops.

A considered process

Privacy is part of how we work.

Our internal procedures cover handling of sensitive files, access decisions, confidentiality expectations, and the escalation of security concerns. These arrangements are reviewed as services and personnel evolve.

No online system can guarantee absolute security. We do not claim an independent security certification or audit.

  • Clear boundaries agreed before an engagement
  • Written approvals for consequential actions
  • Restricted storage for private records
  • Information shared only for approved purposes
  • A direct point of contact for privacy enquiries
