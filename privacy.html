Privacy Policy

How TalentBuzz Private handles information shared by website visitors and prospective private clients.

Effective date: 16 November 2026

This notice describes the current website and enquiry process. Additional service-specific privacy terms may be provided before a private-client engagement begins.

1. Who we are

TalentBuzz Private is a private lifestyle and personal operations service led by Michelle Giray, based in the Philippines and serving prospective clients internationally. For questions about personal information, contact private@talentbuzz.net.

2. Information we collect

When you choose to contact us, your message may include your name, email address, location, service interests and any details you voluntarily provide. We may also receive information in subsequent correspondence or consultations.

The current consultation form on this website does not store your entries in a website database or send them automatically. It opens a prepared message in your own email application. Your information reaches us only if you send that email.

Our website host and third-party resource providers may automatically process technical information such as IP address, browser details, request times and device information as part of delivering the site.

3. Why we use information

We use information you send to us to:

  • Respond to enquiries and arrange consultations;
  • Understand your requested services and assess whether we can assist;
  • Prepare proposals and, where agreed, administer a client relationship;
  • Maintain records needed for security, legal obligations and legitimate business administration.

Depending on the context and applicable law, these activities may rely on steps requested before entering into an agreement, performance of an agreement, legitimate interests or another lawful basis. Where consent is required, we will seek it.

We do not sell personal contact information. We do not use enquiry details for unrelated marketing without an appropriate basis or required permission.

4. When information may be shared

Access to personal information should be limited to authorized TalentBuzz Private personnel and service providers who need it for an agreed purpose. We may disclose necessary information to approved third-party professionals or vendors when coordinating a service at your instruction, or when lawfully required.

Because our operations are based in the Philippines and clients may be located abroad, information may be handled across borders. Any service engagement involving sensitive personal information or third parties will require appropriate contractual and security arrangements.

The current website loads fonts from Google Fonts and decorative photographs from Unsplash. Requests for those resources are made to their respective providers and may involve technical data under their own privacy practices. We have not intentionally embedded advertising trackers or a website analytics platform in the supplied HTML.

5. Retention and security

We aim to keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant enquiry or engagement, subject to applicable legal, accounting and dispute-related requirements. Retention arrangements for active clients will be addressed in the applicable service documentation.

We seek to use reasonable organizational and technical safeguards appropriate to the information involved. No email or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Please do not submit passwords, banking details, medical records, government identification numbers or other highly sensitive information through the initial enquiry.

6. Your privacy rights

Depending on the laws that apply to you and to our processing activities, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability or withdrawal of consent, and to lodge a complaint with a relevant data protection authority.

To make a request, email private@talentbuzz.net with the subject “Privacy Request.” We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, and some legal exceptions may apply.

7. Children

This website is intended for adults seeking private services. We do not knowingly solicit personal data directly from children through this site. If a family service involves a child, additional appropriate authorization and safeguards will be considered before processing related information.

8. Updates to this policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy when our services, tools or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will indicate the current version. Material changes to client data practices may require additional notification.

Questions about privacy?

Contact TalentBuzz Private at private@talentbuzz.net. Please use the subject “Privacy Enquiry.”
